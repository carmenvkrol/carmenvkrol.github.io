This is where I write about accessibility in real codebases: what the WCAG
criteria mean once you are looking at an actual component, which patterns
survive contact with a design system, and where the tooling quietly lets you
down.

## Why there are no comments

Comment sections need moderating, and an unmoderated one becomes someone
else's content on your domain. More practically: most of what people want to
say about a post is something they would rather say directly. So every post
ends with a way to reach me instead, and I read all of it.

If you want to follow along, there is [an RSS feed](/rss.xml).

## What to expect

- Notes from audits, with the identifying details removed
- Component patterns, written out properly rather than linked to
- The occasional argument about something the field has settled on too quickly

Start with whatever looks relevant. If something here is wrong, I would like
to know.
